# Tax source register

Checked on 6 November 2026 from this environment. A publication date, an index date, a file timestamp, and a legal effective date are different facts. None of the rows below is a rate for Ahmad Solution Ltd. No rate is seeded from this register.

The PDFs were not copied into Git. The checksum is SHA-256 of the bytes downloaded on the check date. Bangla sentences quoted below were read from a rendered page. A lossy text extraction is not treated as a certified transcription.

## How to read a row

- **Index date** is the date the NBR or commissionerate HTML showed in its table.
- **Instrument date** is the date printed on the gazette or notification itself.
- **File dates** are the PDF's own creation metadata and, where the server sent one, `Last-Modified`.
- **Effective date** is the date the instrument says it takes force. If that sentence was not read, the effective date is unverified.

## What this register refuses to do

- It does not treat 15 percent as the company's output VAT. The 15 percent figures that were read sit inside SRO 140's withholding and garden-supply clauses, and inside other service lines that were not adopted.
- It does not infer a rate from the company's trade-licence activity.
- It does not treat the 2019 Mushak 6.3 PDF as the form prescribed after SRO 125 took effect on 1 July 2026.
- It does not implement Mushak 6.6. The inspected forms index did not list it, and SRO 140 did not set out that form.
- It does not choose between SRO 210 and SRO 273, and it does not copy either schedule.

## Open questions for an accountant or lawyer

These questions block statutory issue. The application can store a source-backed rule and a working paper. It cannot issue a Mushak 6.3 tax invoice, a VDS certificate, or an AIT certificate while they are open.

1. Is the PDF in row 1, checksum `c09e8538…cf5a2`, still the prescribed Mushak 6.3 artwork after rule 40 was amended with effect from 1 July 2026? SRO 125's extracted amendment of rule 40 mentions Mushak 6.3 and changes clause (গ), and the PDF cites clauses (গ) and (চ), but SRO 125 does not contain a new 6.3 specimen.
2. Which supply or service classification applies to each kind of supply Ahmad Solution Ltd. actually makes, and which effective-dated rate, exemption, or withholding rule applies to that classification? The trade licence is not an answer.
3. For VDS, which direction applies to which contract: a customer withholding from this company, or this company deducting from a supplier? Which certificate or treasury-challan form is required after SRO 140, and does Mushak 6.6 have any role?
4. For income-tax withholding, which of SRO 210 and SRO 273 is the rule in force for a given payment date, did SRO 273 repeal SRO 210, and which official certificate or statement is required? The category-specific schedule must be matched to the payment. It must not be applied to every receipt or supplier payment.
5. The NBR VAT forms portal was not reached because its TLS certificate did not verify here. If that portal holds a later Mushak 6.3, it replaces row 1.
